The author

Hi, I’m Jitendra.

I’m Jitendra Kulkarni — the person behind Knowbase, and the one who wrote every chapter in these manuals.

I didn’t set out to write textbooks. I set out to write the kind of book I’d want in my own hands: one that explains a thing by showing how its parts talk to each other, and never makes you feel slow for asking.

These books are for everyone still finding their footing — especially the lost souls trying to find comfort on two wheels. If a chapter helped you, I’d love to hear about it.

Questions

The short answers.

Is it really free?

Yes, for now. All 150 chapters of Anatomy of a Motorcycle are free to read until Sunday 20 December. After that the full manual is $19 (US dollars).

Why is it free right now?

A new shelf has no readers yet, and I’d rather you judge the book than the pitch. Read as much as you like, and if it helps, tell one person who rides.

How much will the other manuals cost?

The Language of Editing opens at a $19 launch price for its first 10 days, then $29. The Sony a6700 Field Manual’s price will be announced when it launches. All prices are in US dollars.

What’s coming next?

The Language of Editing, a manual on cutting, color and sound through DaVinci Resolve, and The Sony a6700 Field Manual for shooters. Both are in progress and shown as coming soon.

Do I need an account?

No account, no login. Bookmarks and reading progress are saved in your own browser.

Where should I start?

At Part I, or use the “Start here” questions above. Every chapter lists what it depends on, so you can jump straight to the one you need.
