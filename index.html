Why Knowbase

Not documentation. Not a course. A field manual, built to be read start to finish.

Written, Not Generated

Every chapter is authored and structured deliberately, one idea building on the last — not templated or auto-assembled.

Engineered, Not Loose

Numbered parts and sections, explicit dependencies, and cross-references throughout — read in order, or jump straight to what you need.

Complete, Not Abandoned

Finished titles stay finished, cover to cover — no half-written series left hanging after chapter three. Anything still in progress is labeled that way, honestly.

Read Anywhere

Bookmarks, reading progress, and dark mode built in — no app to install, no account required to read.

01 — Original Diagrams

Every Diagram Drawn to Explain, Not Decorate.

49 original technical diagrams across both books, each one built for a single specific concept — a node graph, a trim overlap, a signal path — not stock art dropped in to break up text.

02 — Cross-Referenced Throughout

Nothing Explained in Isolation.

Every chapter states what it depends on and what depends on it. Read start to finish, or jump straight to the one chapter you need — the cross-references make sure you land with the right context either way.

03 — Two Complete Manuals

Finished, Cover to Cover.

150 chapters on motorcycles, 95 on editing in DaVinci Resolve — both fully published, not ongoing series. Every chapter is free to read, cover to cover.

Questions

The short answers.

Is it really free?

Yes. Every chapter of every book on the shelf is free to read — currently Anatomy of a Motorcycle (150 chapters) and The Language of Editing (95 chapters), both complete.

Do I need to create an account?

No account, no login. Bookmarks and reading progress are saved in your own browser.

Where should I start?

At Part I of either book. Each chapter builds on the one before it, but every chapter also lists what it depends on, so you can jump straight to the one you need.

Who writes these?

I do — Jitendra Kulkarni. More motorcycle content lives on Instagram at @2sloowwww.
