About

Who is behind it.

Knowbase is written and illustrated by Jitendra Kulkarni. The aim is books that explain a thing by showing how its parts talk to each other, and never make you feel slow for asking. Anatomy of a Motorcycle is the first manual, and it is complete. The Language of Editing and The Sony a6700 Field Manual are next.
